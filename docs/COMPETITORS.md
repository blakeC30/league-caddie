# Competitive Analysis — One-and-Done Fantasy Golf

Last updated: March 2026

## Direct Competitors (OAD League Management)

These platforms let commissioners create and manage private one-and-done leagues.

| Platform | OAD | Price | Live Scoring | Playoffs | Mobile | Multipliers |
|----------|-----|-------|-------------|----------|--------|-------------|
| **League Caddie** | Yes | $29.99–$149.99/yr | Yes (real-time) | Yes | Web (mobile-first) | Yes (configurable) |
| **Pro Tour Fantasy Golf** (protourfantasygolf.com) | Yes | $180+ flat | Delayed 3-4hrs | No | Responsive web | No |
| **Buzz Fantasy Golf** (buzzfantasygolf.com) | Yes | $60+ league + $2-5/mo per member | Yes (paid only) | No | No | No |

### Pro Tour Fantasy Golf (protourfantasygolf.com)

- **Format**: OAD + Three other formats (Two & Thru, Pick 3 Classic, Let It Ride)
- **Pricing**: $180 for up to 30 teams ($6/team). Additional teams $6 each up to 100, then $1/team after 100. Payment via check, Venmo, or PayPal. No free tier.
- **Features**: Commissioner control over schedule/prizes/lineups. Projected earnings after each round. Built-in charts and statistics. Historical season data. Automated lineup reminder emails. Clubhouse message board. Weekly golfer pick recommendations.
- **Limitations**: Standings update 3-4 hours after rounds (NOT real-time). No free tier. Payment by check/Venmo feels dated. No mobile app.
- **Reputation**: Operating since 2008 — longest-running platform in this space. Owner-operated.
- **Threat level**: MEDIUM-HIGH — established and trusted, but aging. No real-time scoring. 6x more expensive than League Caddie Starter for a similar-sized league.

### Buzz Fantasy Golf (buzzfantasygolf.com)

- **Format**: OAD + salary cap, tiered selection, draft/auction, weighted selection
- **Pricing**: Complex tiered system. Commissioner league fees: $60+ (tiered by team count). Individual memberships: Free (very limited), Standard ($1.99-$2.99/mo), Premium ($3.49-$4.99/mo). 15-day commissioner trial, 7-day membership trial.
- **Features**: Most format variety of any platform. 6 scoring options (earnings, strokes, place finished, to-par, DFS, custom). Live scoring (paid members only). Import teams from CSV. Commissioner tools: email notifications, roster modification. Fantasy research data and ownership statistics.
- **Limitations**: Confusing pricing model. Free members are severely restricted. Live scoring locked behind paywall. No native mobile app.
- **Reputation**: Feature-rich but the pricing complexity is a UX problem. Self-promotes aggressively.
- **Threat level**: MEDIUM-HIGH — most features overall, but bad UX around pricing. Per-member fees add up quickly.

### FGLWeb (fglweb.com)

- **Format**: Draft and salary cap. No OAD template.
- **Pricing**: $125 flat regardless of league size. 4-week free trial.
- **Features**: Commissioner league management. Scoring: earnings, FedEx Cup points, custom.
- **Limitations**: Outdated interface. No OAD format. Reported security concerns with private leagues. No mobile app.
- **Reputation**: Ranked last in comparison reviews. Legacy product.
- **Threat level**: LOW.

### Golf Game Central (golfgamecentral.com)

- **Format**: "Pick one or more PGA players each week" — OAD possible depending on commissioner rules.
- **Pricing**: Completely free.
- **Features**: Free private league creation. Commissioner tools (message board, invites, schedule customization). Multi-league support. Public Fantasy Challenge with sponsor prizes.
- **Limitations**: No live scoring. Feature set appears basic. No mobile app. Limited customization.
- **Reputation**: The main draw is that it's free. Running 2026 season.
- **Threat level**: LOW-MEDIUM — free is compelling, but feature-light. No real-time scoring or automation.

## Real-Money Contest Platforms (Not Direct Competitors)

These are paid contest platforms where users enter pools for cash prizes. Different business model from private league management.

| Platform | Entry Fees | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| **One & Done / Broken Tee Society** (onedone.golf) | $115 | Public OAD pool, not a private league tool. Weekly + segment + season prizes from pot. Endorsed by The Golfer's Journal. |
| **Fantasy Golf Championships** (SHGN) | $25–$1,000 | Guaranteed prize pools ($2.5K–$45K+). Real-time scoring. Pat Mayo endorsement. 36 US states. |
| **Splash Sports** | $100–$250 | $1.25M World Championship. Segment-based payouts. iOS/Android. US + Canada. |
| **Underdog Fantasy** | Varies | $1.2M guaranteed OAD contest. Top 750 paid. Native apps. |
| **DraftKings** | Varies | Primarily daily fantasy, not season-long OAD. Inconsistent OAD availability. |
| **FanDuel** | Varies | Snake draft golf only. No dedicated OAD format. |

These platforms serve a different market (real-money gambling) and are not competing for private league commissioners.

## Major Platforms (Yahoo, ESPN, CBS)

All three have effectively abandoned fantasy golf:
- **Yahoo**: Daily fantasy golf only, no season-long leagues
- **ESPN**: Minimal fantasy golf product, no OAD
- **CBS Sports**: Discontinued fantasy golf entirely
- **PGA Tour Fantasy**: Free pick'em format, unclear if OAD is still supported. Web only.

None offer private OAD league management. This space is wide open.

## Where League Caddie Wins

1. **Playoffs** — no competitor offers bracket-style playoffs for OAD leagues
2. **Configurable multipliers** — 2x majors, 1.5x signature events, or any custom value per tournament
3. **Pricing** — $29.99 for 20 members vs. $180 (Pro Tour) or $125 (FGLWeb). 5-6x cheaper.
4. **Real-time scoring** — Pro Tour is delayed 3-4 hours. Buzz locks live scoring behind a per-member paywall.
5. **Modern mobile-first UI** — incumbents look dated. League Caddie matches the UX standard set by Better than Most.
6. **No per-member fees** — Buzz charges $2-5/month per member on top of commissioner fees. League Caddie is one flat price.
7. **Multi-league support** — one account, up to 5 leagues
8. **No-pick penalty** — configurable per league. Most platforms don't penalize missed weeks.

## Where Competitors Win

1. **Pro Tour Fantasy Golf** — 18 years of trust, multiple format variants, weekly pick recommendations
2. **Buzz Fantasy Golf** — most format variety (6 scoring options, 5+ league formats), research data
3. **Golf Game Central** — completely free for basic leagues

## The Real Competitor

**Google Sheets.** Forum posts on GolfWRX explicitly say commissioners can't find affordable OAD tools and resort to spreadsheets. The thread "Anyone know a good One and Done fantasy app?" laments that the only option is $125+. League Caddie at $29.99 directly answers this pain point — replacing hours of weekly spreadsheet work for less than the cost of a round of golf.

---

## Market Gaps League Caddie Fills

1. **No affordable private OAD league platform exists** — the market is split between "free but no features" (Golf Game Central) and "$125-180 per league" (FGLWeb, Pro Tour). The most prominent OAD-branded site (onedone.golf) is actually a $115 public pool, not a private league tool at all.
2. **No platform offers OAD playoffs** — this is entirely unique
3. **No platform offers configurable tournament multipliers** for OAD
4. **No modern, mobile-first OAD league manager** exists — the incumbents are desktop-era web apps
5. **The "spreadsheet replacement" market is large and underserved** — many leagues still run on Google Sheets/Forms because existing tools are too expensive or too rigid
